Privacy Policy

SimpleFlights  ·  Effective date: May 13, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 13, 2025

Plain-language summary: SimpleFlights stores all your data locally on your device. We do not operate accounts, do not run advertising, and do not share your information with third parties. The only time data leaves your device is when you choose to share a live flight with another person.

1. Who We Are

SimpleFlights is developed and published by Aaron Katz, an individual developer based in Ontario, Canada ("we", "us", or "our"). Questions about this policy can be sent to aaron@simpleflights.ca.

2. Information We Collect

SimpleFlights collects only the information necessary to provide its core features.

Location Data

When you start an active flight session, the app requests access to your device's GPS location. This data is used to:

  • Display your real-time position on the moving map
  • Record your flight path, altitude, speed, and heading
  • Calculate distances and estimated times to planned waypoints

Location data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server automatically.

Flight Logs & Aircraft Data

Flight logs (route, timestamps, altitude, speed) and any aircraft profiles you create are stored locally using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device unless you initiate a sharing session (see Section 4).

App Preferences

Settings such as demo mode state and in-app notification preferences are stored locally using your device's standard key-value storage. No preference data is transmitted anywhere.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account creation or collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track usage behaviour, session data, or in-app events.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data.

4. Live Flight Sharing

SimpleFlights offers two optional, user-initiated sharing modes:

Local Wi-Fi Sharing

Your live flight data is broadcast over your local Wi-Fi network so that another person on the same network can follow along. Data does not leave your local network and is not routed through any server.

Remote Sharing via MQTT

When you enable remote sharing, your live flight telemetry (position, altitude, speed, heading) is transmitted to an MQTT message broker operated and self-hosted by Aaron Katz in Ontario, Canada, solely for the purpose of relaying that data to the recipient you share your code with. Data in transit is not stored persistently on the broker; it is relayed in real-time and discarded. The session ends when you stop sharing.

You can revoke sharing at any time by tapping the stop-sharing button in the app.

5. How We Use Information

All information collected by SimpleFlights is used exclusively to:

  • Display the moving map and flight statistics within the app
  • Record and replay your flight history
  • Relay live telemetry to a recipient you have explicitly chosen

We do not use any collected information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than those listed above.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because data is stored on your device, you control retention entirely. Flight logs and aircraft profiles can be deleted at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Data that passes through the MQTT relay broker is not stored and therefore requires no deletion request.

7. Children's Privacy

SimpleFlights is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights (PIPEDA & Applicable Privacy Law)

SimpleFlights is operated in compliance with Canada's federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where applicable, provincial privacy legislation including Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

Because SimpleFlights does not collect, store, or process personal data on any server controlled by us, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, port, or erase. All data resides on your own device and is under your direct control.

Users in the European Economic Area benefit from equivalent protections consistent with the GDPR. We do not sell personal information under any jurisdiction's definition.

If you have questions or believe we have inadvertently received personal data, contact aaron@simpleflights.ca and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Services

SimpleFlights does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social login, or data-brokering services. Map tile data is rendered on-device from standard aviation chart sources; no user-identifiable data is sent with tile requests.

10. Security

We follow platform-standard security practices. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (passcode, Face ID, etc.). Live-sharing sessions use standard network transport; we recommend using trusted networks when sharing flight data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
